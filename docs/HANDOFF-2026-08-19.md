## Objective #1: beat MedCPT. Two untested levers, both live

1. **Objective mismatch** — trained on CITE doc-doc pairs, evaluated query→doc. 424,753
   query-shaped pairs (`cshl-query-pairs`) were built and never used.
2. **Batch starvation** — trained at batch 4 after the OOM guard halved it. Contrastive learning
   scales with in-batch negatives; batch 4 gives ~3. This was identified the day after and is
   independent of (1). See `RIG-DOCTRINE.md` §1.

**Staged next run** (approved in principle, not fired): 40,000 pairs stratified across
MESH + AUTHORKW + EXTRACT. Amendment on the table: run it on a **multi-GPU box** (g5.12xlarge,
48 vCPU — fits the new 64-vCPU on-demand quota) for effective batch 16 and ~4× shorter wall clock.
Ceiling would need raising past $10.50. If it wins, ablate afterwards to learn which lever did it.
